Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026. Template language, to be reviewed by an attorney before launch.

What we collect

When an employee completes a mission, we collect the name and company they enter, the mission, score, completion time, date, and certificate code. When you contact us, we collect what you type in the form.

Why

We use completion records only to give license holders proof of training and to support our customers. We use contact information only to respond to you.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, we do not use advertising trackers, and missions do not require accounts or passwords.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store card numbers.

Retention and requests

Completion records are kept for the license year plus three years so customers can show training history. To see, correct, or delete your information, email us.
