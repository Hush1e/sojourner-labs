Privacy Policy

Public-demo data handling · Updated November 22, 2026.

Current public experience

Mission display names, organization names, scores, and certificate codes are processed in your browser. The public configuration has no automatic completion-report endpoint. Optional email sharing opens your email application and sends only if you choose to send.

Contact and hosting

The contact form prepares an email draft. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; hosting and email providers process information according to their own policies. Do not enter confidential workplace information into the demonstrations.

Organizational delivery

Payment processing and automatic organizational reporting are not enabled in the current public configuration. Data handling for a future organizational deployment must be agreed before activation.
