## The Problem

Small and midsize businesses face the same threats as large companies with a fraction of the staff to manage them. Most breaches begin with an employee action, such as clicking a phishing link or sending sensitive data the wrong way. At the same time, employees have started using free AI tools to write emails and summarize documents, often without any guidance on what information is safe to paste into them. Cyber insurance applications, client contracts, and state privacy laws increasingly ask whether a business trains its people. Many businesses either have no training in place or rely on slide decks that employees click through without reading.

## The Solution

The The Brightline Breach is a short, interactive program built around three everyday tasks: reading email, using AI assistants, and sharing files. Each module teaches one practical habit and then asks the employee to apply it to a realistic scenario. An employee cannot move forward until they answer correctly, which means completion reflects understanding rather than clicks.

| Module | What Employees Learn | Interactive Challenge |
|---|---|---|
| Cybersecurity Awareness | Warning signs of phishing: look-alike sender domains, false urgency, suspicious links, and how to report safely | Inspect an urgent email and identify where it really came from |
| AI Awareness | The Placeholder Method for using AI tools without exposing customer or company information, and checking AI output for errors | Choose the one AI prompt that is safe to use |
| Compliance | What counts as protected personal information and how to share it securely with auditors, vendors, and partners | Pick the file sharing method that protects customer data |

## Why It Supports Your Compliance Efforts

Ohio's Data Protection Act (Ohio Revised Code Chapter 1354) gives businesses an affirmative defense in certain data breach lawsuits when they maintain a written cybersecurity program that reasonably conforms to a recognized framework, such as the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. Workforce awareness and training is a core element of those frameworks. The The Brightline Breach is designed to support the training portion of your program and to give you documentation that training took place. It does not by itself make a business compliant with any law, and we recommend confirming your specific obligations with your legal counsel or insurance carrier.
