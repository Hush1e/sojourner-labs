The Workforce Risk Shield

1. Cybersecurity Awareness

Most small business breaches start with a person, not a computer. Employees inspect a realistic phishing email, learn the warning signs that matter, and practice what to do before anyone clicks.

2. AI Awareness

Your staff already uses AI tools to write emails and summarize notes. This module teaches the Placeholder Method: how to get help from AI without pasting customer names, account numbers, or company plans into a tool you do not control.

3. Compliance

State privacy laws and cyber insurance carriers expect businesses to protect personal information and train their people. Employees learn what counts as protected data and how to share it safely with partners, auditors, and vendors.

About the Company

Practical Training Built by an Educator

Vanguard Cyber Labs builds short, interactive security training for organizations that do not have a security department. Each module is written in plain language for everyday employees and is built around tasks they already do: reading email, using AI assistants, and sharing files. Every challenge is locked, so an employee must solve the scenario correctly before moving forward. Training runs in any standard web browser with nothing to install and no accounts to create.

Meet the Founder

Norma DePriest

Norma DePriest is a cybersecurity educator and curriculum designer who leads a college cybersecurity degree program in Ohio. She took over the program in 2024, rebuilt its full curriculum of fourteen courses, and designed a new Artificial Intelligence certificate that pairs AI skills with security fundamentals. Her work centers on turning technical security concepts into training that non-technical learners can understand and apply on the job.

Norma serves on the Ohio Department of Higher Education's Ohio Guaranteed Transfer Pathways cybersecurity committee and leads an employer advisory partnership that keeps her program aligned with what businesses in the region need. She holds a Master's degree in Cybersecurity Engineering and a Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, and she is pursuing a Doctor of Education in Instructional Design and Technology, where her research focuses on adaptive learning systems that measure real gains in learner performance.
