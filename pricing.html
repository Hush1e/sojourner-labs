Questions

How long does a mission take?

Most learners finish in 15 to 25 minutes.

Does this count for cyber insurance?

Many carriers ask whether staff receive security awareness training. Each learner's certificate gives you a dated record. Confirm requirements with your carrier.

How do I track who finished?

Every completion is recorded with name, score, date, and certificate code, and you receive a completion report. See How It Works.

Do employees need accounts?

No. Missions run in any modern browser with no installs.

Can you build a mission around our business?

Yes. Custom scenarios are available. Ask when you request a license.
