DESIGNED BY NORMA DEPRIEST

Realistic decisions. Purposeful play.

I design immersive, gamified AI and cybersecurity experiences to help people prepare for the workforce.

From workplace use cases to playable practice

Sojourner Labs turns phishing, impersonation, AI use, account security, and incident response into interactive workplace scenarios. Learners inspect evidence, make decisions, receive feedback, and reflect on the consequences.

My framework-informed approach includes NIST NICE: connecting experiences to the tasks, knowledge, and skills people need in the workforce. Framework alignment informs design; it is not an external endorsement or certification.

Professional background

Program Director of Cybersecurity at Hocking College, NCL Founding Players Committee member and Live Show Coordinator, and an EdD student in Instructional Design and Technology at South College.

Earned degrees include an M.S. in Cybersecurity (2022), B.A. in General Studies (2019), and A.S. in Information Technology (2018).
