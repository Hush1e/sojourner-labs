Meet the founder

ND

Norma DePriest is the Program Director of Cybersecurity at a public college in Ohio, where she rebuilt all 14 courses in the cybersecurity program in 2024 and designed the college's new AI certificate. She serves on the Ohio Department of Higher Education OGTP Cybersecurity Committee and leads an employer advisory partnership that keeps her curriculum tied to what local businesses need. Her work sits at the meeting point of security, artificial intelligence, and how adults actually learn.

She holds an M.S. in Cybersecurity Engineering and a B.S. in Information Technology, and she is a doctoral student in Instructional Design and Technology. Sojourner Labs brings that classroom experience to small businesses that need training people will finish and remember. Every mission is built on the same principle she uses with her students: people learn security by doing it.
