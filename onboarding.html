How it works

Most businesses are fully rolled out in one afternoon.

  1. Choose your license

    Pick the tier that fits your team size. Payment is a single annual charge, and you receive a confirmation by email.

  2. Get your company link

    We send you a personal link for your company, for example …/missions.html?org=YourCompany, so certificates carry your company name.

  3. Send it to your team

    Forward the link with a short note. We include a ready to send announcement email. Nothing to install and no accounts to create.

  4. Track completion

    Every finished mission is recorded with the employee's name, score, date, and certificate code. You receive a completion report, and any certificate can be checked on our verification page.

  5. Keep your records

    Save the report for your cyber insurance renewal, audits, or your written security program. Missions can be replayed any time during your license year.
