BRIEFING / ACTION / REFLECTION

Experience the work.

01 · Take a role

Choose a mission and read your workplace briefing. Use a display name or nickname for your completion certificate. Do not enter sensitive workplace information.

02 · Inspect and decide

Review messages, logs, policies, or incident evidence. Make a decision, use a contextual hint if needed, and see simulated consequences. You can recover from incorrect answers.

03 · Review your record

Complete the mission to review your choices, score, and debrief. Replay to practice a different approach.

04 · Keep your certificate

Print or save the browser-generated certificate. Optional email sharing opens your email application; you decide whether to send it. The public version does not automatically submit employer completion reports.
